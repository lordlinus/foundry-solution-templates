# Hypertension

Illustrative guideline for a sample template. Not actuarial guidance.

## Hypertension: controlled on treatment
Applies when at least two readings in the last 12 months are below 140/90 mmHg on stable treatment.
Decision: Standard.

## Hypertension: mildly elevated
Applies when the most recent readings are 140-159 systolic or 90-99 diastolic, with or without treatment.
Decision: Rated (moderate loading). Postpone if treatment started within the last 3 months.

## Hypertension: severe or with organ involvement
Applies to readings of 160/100 mmHg or higher, or hypertension with documented kidney, heart, or eye involvement.
Decision: Refer to underwriter.
