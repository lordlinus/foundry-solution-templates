# Raised cholesterol

Illustrative guideline for a sample template. Not actuarial guidance.

## Raised cholesterol: treated or mild
Applies when total cholesterol is below 7.5 mmol/L (290 mg/dL), treated or untreated, with no other cardiovascular risk factor.
Decision: Standard.

## Raised cholesterol: with other risk factors
Applies when raised cholesterol occurs with hypertension, diabetes, or smoking.
Decision: Rated (low loading), assessed together with the other conditions.
