# Thyroid nodule

Illustrative guideline for a sample template. Not actuarial guidance.

## Thyroid nodule: benign
Applies when ultrasound or biopsy reports a benign nodule and thyroid function is normal.
Decision: Standard.

## Thyroid nodule: under investigation
Applies when a biopsy or follow-up imaging is ordered or pending.
Decision: Postpone until the result is reported.
