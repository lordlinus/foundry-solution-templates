# Type 2 diabetes

Illustrative guideline for a sample template. Not actuarial guidance.

## Type 2 diabetes: well controlled
Applies when HbA1c is below 7.0% on the two most recent results and there are no documented complications.
Decision: Rated (low loading).

## Type 2 diabetes: moderately controlled
Applies when the most recent HbA1c is 7.0-8.5%.
Decision: Rated (moderate loading).

## Type 2 diabetes: poorly controlled or complicated
Applies when HbA1c is above 8.5%, or kidney disease, retinopathy, or neuropathy is documented.
Decision: Refer to underwriter.

## Type 2 diabetes: insufficient evidence
Applies when no HbA1c result from the last 12 months is reported.
Decision: Postpone until a current HbA1c is available.
